# SpendWise Play Store Listing Details

## App Name (27/30 chars):
SpendWise - Expense Tracker

## Short Description (73/80 chars):
Track daily expenses, manage Khata ledger & set budgets with 100% privacy.

## Full Description:
Take complete control of your personal finances with SpendWise — a fast, modern, and 100% offline personal expense manager, budget planner, and digital Khata ledger. 

SpendWise keeps all your financial records safely on your phone without selling your data, showing intrusive ads, or requiring cloud logins.

🌟 KEY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 SMART EXPENSE & INCOME TRACKING
• Log expenses and incomes in seconds with customized categories.
• Visual spending breakdowns, daily trends, and interactive monthly charts.
• Filter transactions instantly by date range, category, payment mode (UPI, Card, Cash), or search by merchant.

🎯 MONTHLY CATEGORY BUDGETS & ALERTS
• Set custom spending limits for Food, Groceries, Shopping, Travel, and more.
• Dynamic Spend-O-Meter gauge warns you before you exceed your budget.
• Real-time notifications when you reach 80% or 100% of your limit.

📒 DIGITAL KHATA BOOK (UDHAR / LEDGER)
• Keep track of money given (Aapko milenge) and money received (Aapko dene hain) with friends, family, and local merchants.
• Clear balance breakdown per contact with full transaction history.
• Export professional PDF ledger statements in one tap.

👥 1-TAP BILL SPLITTING & WHATSAPP REMINDERS
• Split restaurant dinners, trip bills, or flat rent equally, by exact amount, or percentage.
• Automatically logs your share as an expense and records your friends' portions directly in Khata.
• Send friendly WhatsApp payment reminders with automatic UPI deep links for instant settlement.

📸 RECEIPT VAULT & BILL ATTACHMENTS
• Snap photos of paper bills, restaurant slips, warranty cards, or UPI screenshots.
• Compressed offline storage saves phone space while preserving crisp clarity.
• Full-screen pinch-to-zoom viewer and instant document sharing.

🔒 BIOMETRIC SECURITY & PRIVACY
• Lock the app with Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or encrypted 4-digit PIN.
• Privacy Mode hides balances and account numbers on the dashboard with 1 tap.
• 100% local database — your financial data never leaves your device.
